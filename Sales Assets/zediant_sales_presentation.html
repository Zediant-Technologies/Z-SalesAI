Section 01 // Policy Standards

Non-Negotiable Commercial Rules

These rules govern every proposal, pricing conversation, and client engagement. They protect our gross margins and eliminate low-probability distractions.

Rate Floor Policy $25–$30 / hr

Blended Hourly Rate Minimum

Never quote below USD 25/hour blended rate. We do not compete with low-tier commodity offshore freelance body shops. We staff senior developers with Australian/Dubai working-hour overlap who integrate into client stand-ups and codebases.

Deal Sizing Policy $10k+ Floor

Minimum Contract Threshold

Minimum fixed-scope entry deal is USD 10,000. Pod engagements must target USD 80,000–$150,000+ annualized. Any request under $5,000 is disqualified—it drains onboarding capacity without generating sustainable margin.

Speculative Work Rule Zero Free Work

No Free POCs or Unpaid Tenders

Explicitly prohibited by internal strategy. Free technical work devalues our authority. Convert any discovery friction into a paid 2-Week Architecture & Integration Diagnostic ($3,000–$5,000) or pre-cleared umbrella teaming agreement.

Public Sector Policy Teaming Agreements Only

Government Tender Guardrail

Chasing standalone government tenders without panel status drains billable capacity through massive uncompensated documentation. Manish will only engage public sector opportunities via formal Teaming Agreements with pre-approved Prime Contractors.

Compliance Stance: Always use SOC 2 Type II aligned in all collateral. Never state "certified" until the audit attestation is delivered.

Section 03 // Channel Playbook

Motion 1: The Agency Whitelabel Engine

The single fastest cashflow engine in IT services. Winning 3 agency partners in Australia and UAE delivers continuous recurring revenue without prospecting end-clients.

The Agency Pain & Offer Fit

The Operational Reality: Agencies win work in sudden bursts. When a $150k client build lands, hiring local full-time engineers takes 2 months and creates ruinous bench cost when the project ends. Freelancers are unvetted and churn mid-sprint.
The Offer: Zero-Risk Teaming Agreement Sign an umbrella NDA and rate schedule ($25–$30/hr) with $0 upfront cost. When their pipeline spikes, Zediant provides an embedded whitelabel dev pod on 5–10 days notice with a 30-day flex scale-down clause.
Why Agency Leaders Say Yes: It costs them nothing today. It acts as an insurance policy that allows their sales reps to bid on larger technical projects with zero capacity risk.

Section 04 // Direct Acquisition

Motion 2: Multi-Trigger SaaS Outbound

Eliminates zero-response cold emails. We never contact a company based on firmographics alone; prospects must exhibit active, verifiable operational friction.

The Multi-Trigger Intent Requirement (Strict Gating)

As strategic review identified, Funding != Propensity to Buy. A company that just raised $5M is often determined to hire an onshore team. We only sequence accounts that show Trigger A (Capital Pressure) + Trigger B (Delivery Friction) simultaneously:

Trigger 1: Recruiting Lag
Senior React, Python, or Cloud roles open for >45 days on Seek/LinkedIn. Proves internal hiring failure.
Trigger 2: Capital & Board Clock
Closed Seed/Series A within last 6 months. High runway burn and investor-committed roadmap deadlines.
Trigger 3: Platform Evolution
Announced v2 rebuild, expanding API integration footprint, or cloud migration from monolith.
The 5-Step Alternating Sequence Cadence
Step 1: The Hook
Personalized Opening + Pain Point fused in 1 paragraph.
Step 2: Authority
Zero Tokens. Roadmap vs. senior bandwidth reality. (Threaded Re:).
Step 3: Deep Proof
Business Challenge + Relevant Zediant Case Study.
Step 4: The Trade-off
Hiring vs. Capacity (recruiting lag vs. breathing room).
Step 5: Breakup
Dignified permission close; prompts easy reply.

Section 11 // Sales Methodology

The Technical Discovery Framework

Used by Manish and Pritamjit on all 30-minute introductory calls. Diagnoses engineering friction, eliminates tire-kickers, and extracts commercial parameters.

Q1: Bottleneck Diagnosis

"When you look at your roadmap over the next two quarters, is the delivery squeeze primarily due to specialized technical gaps, or sheer senior developer bandwidth?"

Separates custom architecture needs from dedicated pod capacity opportunities.

Q2: Hiring Lag Quantification

"I noticed you've had a Senior {{Stack}} role open for some time. What has been the primary barrier with candidates so far—compensation mismatch, technical caliber, or candidate drop-off?"

Quantifies recruiting cost and urgency; positions immediate embedded pods.

Q3: Codebase & Team Integration

"How does your engineering team typically integrate external capacity? Do you have an established PR review and CI/CD workflow that embedded developers plug into?"

Validates operational maturity and confirms stand-up / timezone alignment.

Q4: Architectural & Integration Footprint

"What existing systems, third-party ERP/CRM platforms, or legacy databases does this application need to communicate with?"

Identifies integration complexity and opportunities for ZCoupler middleware.

Q5: Decision Clock & Deadlines

"What is the hard milestone or board-committed release date driving this project? What happens if that deadline slips by 60 days?"

Establishes executive urgency and consequences of inaction.

Q6: Commercial & Budget Fit

"Most engineering teams we partner with deploy a dedicated 2–3 engineer pod at $25–$30/hour blended rate. Does that structure align with your budget envelope?"

Enforces commercial rate floor before committing proposal engineering time.

Logging Rule: Call notes answering these 6 questions must be logged in Zoho CRM within 2 hours of call completion. Stage: Solutioning

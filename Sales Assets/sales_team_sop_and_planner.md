**Document Version:** 2.0 (Operational Blueprint)  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Governing Objective:** Diversify revenue, reduce dominant client concentration from ~90% to below 40% within 12–18 months, and establish predictable monthly recurring revenue (MRR) through dedicated pods, staff augmentation, and partner channels.

---

## 1. Executive Context & Rules of Engagement

To eliminate micro-management, every team member must operate with full autonomy within clearly documented guardrails.

### Commercial Guardrails
1. **No Free Speculative Work:** Never provide unpaid proof-of-concepts (POCs), free code samples, or unpaid architectural roadmaps. Conversion must be to a paid diagnostic (\$3,000–\$5,000) or pre-cleared umbrella agreement.
2. **Pricing Floor:** Minimum blended rate floor of **USD 25–30/hour** for offshore engineering pods. Minimum project/entry deal size: **USD 10,000**.
3. **Government Tender Policy:** Public sector bids requiring physical presence, unpaid documentation, or on-shore clearance must **only** be pursued through a formal **Teaming Agreement** with an established prime contractor. Standalone unvetted tender pursuit is strictly deprioritized.
4. **Compliance Language:** Always state **"SOC 2 Type II aligned"** in all messaging and collateral. Never claim "certified" until the final attestation report is in hand.

## 5. The Handoff & Escalation Protocol (Zero Drop-Off)

To ensure prospects move seamlessly through the pipeline without miscommunication:

```
                               LEAD PROGRESSION PIPELINE
[Stage 1: Sourcing]    ──> Apollo multi-trigger qualification (Pritamjit verifies).
[Stage 2: Outreach]    ──> Automated email sequence + LinkedIn touchpoint (Manish / Pritamjit).
[Stage 3: Discovery]   ──> 30-min discovery call led by Manish (Direct) or Pritamjit (Agency).
[Stage 4: Solutioning] ──> Brief logged in Zoho; Rajeev drafts Proposal / Teaming Agreement.
[Stage 5: Closing]     ──> Commercial review call led by Rajeev & Manish; contract signed.
[Stage 6: Onboarding]  ──> Pod kicked off within 5–10 days; delivery lead assigned.
```

### Immediate Escalation Triggers (Notify Rajeev within 2 hours):
1. Any enterprise prospect requesting an MSA for 3+ dedicated developers.
2. Any agency requesting a Master Teaming Agreement for upcoming quarterly client work.
3. Any question regarding **SOC 2 Type II compliance reports**, security questionnaires, or custom SLAs.
4. Any deal requiring custom pricing or scale-down terms outside standard thresholds.

## 6. How to Run This Without Micro-Management

1. **The CRM is the Single Source of Truth:** If an activity, discovery call, or deal stage is not updated in **Zoho CRM**, it did not happen. No verbal updates.
2. **Weekly Scorecard Review (Friday 4:30 PM, 20 Mins):**
   - Each partner reports only their 3 primary numbers:
     - **Pritamjit:** Agencies Contacted | Upwork Bids | Discovery Calls Booked
     - **Manish:** CTO Discovery Calls | Client Interviews Booked | Proposals Requested
     - **Rajeev:** Proposals Issued | Deals Closed | Deliverability Health
3. **Red Flag Thresholds:** If any leading indicator KPI falls below 75% of target for two consecutive weeks, the process is reviewed on Monday to remove bottlenecks immediately.

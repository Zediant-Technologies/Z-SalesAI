## Executive Overview & Commercial Imperatives

This document establishes the official Go-To-Market (GTM) operating model, role accountabilities, daily/weekly task cadence, and performance scorecards for Zediant Technologies. It is designed to provide complete operational clarity across the leadership team (**Rajeev Jaiswal**, **Manish Bhaskar**, **Pritamjit Banerjee**, and support staff **Shreya**), enabling high-autonomy execution without micro-management.

### 1. The Strategic Mandate
* **Current Operational Baseline:** Zediant operates as a 25–35 person specialized software engineering consultancy operating at break-even, with approximately **90% of revenue concentrated in a single client relationship**.
* **Delivery Constraint:** Zediant can sustainably onboard **5–7 additional senior developers within a 3-month window**. Uncontrolled sales volume that exceeds this threshold creates operational failure; insufficient volume perpetuates existential concentration risk.
* **12–18 Month Target:** Diversify client revenue such that **no single account represents more than 40% of total revenue**, achieved through high-retention dedicated engineering pods, white-label agency partnerships, and high-margin integration projects.

### 2. Commercial Rules of Engagement (Non-Negotiable)

| Policy Area | Governing Rule | Operational Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pricing Floor** | **USD 25–30 / hour blended** for engineering pods. | Protects gross margins above delivery cost; avoids competing against low-end commodity body shops. |
| **Minimum Deal Size** | **USD 10,000** for fixed-scope entry work; **USD 80,000–150,000+** annualized for dedicated pods. | Engagements below USD 5,000 consume disproportionate onboarding overhead without margin viability. |
| **Speculative Work** | **Zero unpaid POCs or tender documentation.** | Free technical work devalues engineering authority. Convert initial scoping into a paid Architecture/Integration Audit ($3,000–$5,000) or pre-cleared umbrella agreement. |
| **Compliance Terminology** | Strictly **"SOC 2 Type II aligned"**. | Prevents legal exposure. Never use "certified" or "externally audited" until formal attestation documentation is complete. |
| **Public Sector Policy** | **Teaming Agreements with Prime Contractors only.** | Uncompensated standalone government tender bidding is explicitly prohibited due to procurement drag. Manish will only pursue public opportunities through established prime vendors. |

## GTM Core Motions & Execution Playbooks

---

### MOTION 1: The Digital Agency Channel Playbook
*Target: Heads of Delivery, Operations Directors, Managing Directors, COOs of Digital Agencies (15–75 employees) in Australia & UAE.*

#### 1. Strategic Rationale
Agencies manage portfolios of 30–100 end-clients. Winning a single agency partner can yield 2–6 concurrent developer placements without ongoing customer acquisition costs.

#### 2. The Commercial Entry: The "Zero-Risk Overflow Teaming Agreement"
Agencies fear two things: (1) getting burned by poor offshore quality, and (2) carrying bench payroll when client projects end. We resolve both upfront:
* **The Structure:** A pre-executed Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Mutual NDA establishing agreed blended hourly rates (**\$25–\$30/hr**), SLA response times, and white-label confidentiality.
* **The Cost:** **\$0 commitment** until a project is activated.
* **The Scale Down:** Clean 30-day scale-down notice.
* **The Position:** Zediant is officially enrolled as their *on-demand engineering bench*. When they win a large contract or suffer developer churn, they tap our bench immediately instead of turning down client work.

#### 3. Standard Agency Outreach Sequence (Pritamjit)
* **Touch 1 (LinkedIn Connection):** Clean peer note referencing their agency’s design or client delivery work.
* **Touch 2 (Email 1 — The Bench Squeeze):**
  > *Hi {{First Name}},*  
  > *Saw the recent digital work {{Company}} has been shipping across {{relevant sector}}. Most agency COOs and delivery leads I speak with in Australia face the same headache: when project wins bunch up, finding senior developers on short notice is tough, and relying on loose freelancers usually leads to quality risks.*  
  > *We act as an on-demand white-label engineering bench for agencies. We put a pre-cleared teaming agreement in place at fixed blended rates, so when your pipeline spikes, you have immediate senior dev pods ready to build under your brand without carrying bench cost when the project wraps.*  
  > *Would it make sense to put an overflow agreement in place so you have capacity on standby for your next client win?*  
  > *Best regards, Pritamjit*
* **Touch 3 (Threaded Follow-up — Re:):** Pure architectural/commercial value explaining our 30-day flex terms, senior-default staffing, and Australian/Dubai timezone overlap.
* **Touch 4 (Breakup Note — Re:):** Dignified close asking if they are covered for the upcoming quarter.

### MOTION 2: The Multi-Trigger Direct SaaS & Product Playbook
*Target: CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Technical Founders of funded software products (20–150 employees).*

#### 1. Strategic Rationale
Direct SaaS clients provide long-term, high-MRR contracts (6–18 month pod retainers). However, cold outreach fails unless timed to an **active operational pain trigger**.

#### 2. The Multi-Trigger Intent Filter
Leads imported from Apollo or LinkedIn must possess **at least two simultaneous verified triggers** before entering active sequences:
1. **Trigger A (Financial Pressure):** Closed Seed, Series A, or Series B round within the past 6 months (board pressure on product delivery).
2. **Trigger B (Hiring Friction):** Senior software engineering roles open for **>45 days** on Seek, LinkedIn, or company career pages (internal recruiting failure).
3. **Trigger C (Architecture Expansion):** Public roadmap commitments, platform rebuilds (monolith to microservices), or expanding third-party API partner programs.

#### 3. The 5-Step Sequence Architecture (Manish & Rajeev)
* **Step 1 (The Hook):** Personalized observation + Specific pain point fused together.
* **Step 2 (The Technical Reality — ZERO Tokens):** Pure thought leadership on roadmap execution (*"It's rarely about knowing what to build; it's finding the senior bandwidth to build it."*). Threaded as `Re:`.
* **Step 3 (The Deep Proof):** Specific Business Challenge + relevant Zediant Case Study (e.g., *11Wickets AWS Galera cluster*, *automotive DMS middleware*, *Zakaa enterprise hub*).
* **Step 4 (Strategic Dilemma — ZERO Tokens):** The pragmatic trade-off between full-time recruiting lag and temporary flexible capacity.
* **Step 5 (The Dignified Close — ZERO Tokens):** Low-friction permission close.

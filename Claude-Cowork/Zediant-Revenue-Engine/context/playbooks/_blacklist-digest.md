# Blacklist Digest — condensed from `zediant_outreach_natural_language_blacklist.md`

*Source: `context/zediant_outreach_natural_language_blacklist.md` · condensed September 2026 for token efficiency · re-check the full file if a genuinely new phrase needs a ruling not covered here.*

Governs four fields: `Email_Personalised_Opening`, `Email_Pain_Points`, `Case_Study`, `Business_Challenges`. This is a **language-quality guide, not a literal spam filter** — never distort a verified fact to dodge a word; rewrite the sentence around it instead. Read this before drafting, not after.

## Core rule
Don't use avoid-list language unless genuinely necessary to state a verified fact. Never stack buzzwords. Prefer plain, specific language that reads as if a person researched the company.

## Avoid — by category (representative, not exhaustive; full list in the source file)
- **Money/urgency/hype:** guaranteed, risk-free, act now, limited time, exclusive, game-changing, world-class, best-in-class, ultimate/perfect/complete solution
- **AI buzzwords:** AI-powered, AI-driven, AI-native, "harness/leverage/unlock the power of AI", AI transformation. **"AI" itself is fine** — say "AI features," "AI integration," "AI initiatives" when genuinely relevant to the prospect's verified activity, never as a framing device
- **Corporate/marketing:** leverage, synergy, optimize, transformation, empower, ecosystem, holistic, end-to-end, best practices, future-proof, disruptive, seamless, streamline, unlock potential/value, drive growth/innovation
- **B2B sales clichés:** "I'd love to connect/explore," "explore synergies/opportunities," "potential/strategic partnership," "see how we can help," "let's jump on a call," "touch base," "circle back"
- **CTA language (personalization fields normally carry no meeting CTA at all):** book/schedule a demo/call/meeting, "let's connect/chat/talk," "do you have 15 minutes," "what does your calendar look like"
- **Generic spam-trigger boilerplate** (gambling, diet/health claims, phishing-style "verify your account," financial-services cold-call language, ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, emoji): none of this is naturally relevant to B2B engineering outreach, so it shouldn't come up — if a draft ever drifts this direction, rewrite it
- **Financial/performance claims:** guaranteed results, "10x faster," "instant results" — unsupported. A real, approved Zediant metric (e.g. an approved "40% faster" case-study figure) may be used; never invent or generalize a client result into a promise about the prospect
- **Formatting:** no em dashes, en dashes, or double hyphens; no ALL CAPS; no multiple exclamation marks; no bold-style emphasis inside a field value

## Human-writing test
Bad: *"I've identified a transformative opportunity to leverage your innovative technology ecosystem."*
Good: *"I noticed the team has been expanding the platform while continuing to build out engineering."*

## Per-field rules
- **Email_Personalised_Opening** — one verified observation: product activity, expansion, hiring, new features/markets, engineering activity, a technology initiative. No generic growth statements.
- **Email_Pain_Points** — one likely pressure, tied to roadmap pressure, engineering capacity, hiring, product complexity, delivery workload, or maintaining existing systems while building new. Hedge ("can," "may," "often") when inferred rather than confirmed.
- **Case_Study** — the most relevant verified Zediant example. Never invent a result, challenge, technology, or scope.
- **Business_Challenges** — the actual verified challenge the case-study client faced, kept distinct from the prospect's own inferred pain point (that's Email_Pain_Points' job).

## Final quality check (run before saving any field)
1. Checked against the avoid-list above (rewritten around, not distorted)
2. Specific to this company or case study, not generic
3. ~15-25 words (Opening, Pain Points, Case_Study); Business_Challenges is one self-contained sentence
4. Reads naturally aloud, no unsupported claims, doesn't sound like marketing copy
5. Fits the exact email slot the merge tag lands in (see each skill's Saleshandy Template Compatibility notes)
6. Doesn't needlessly repeat another field

**Closing test:** *"Would this sound normal if a Zediant salesperson personally typed this email to one CTO?"* If not, rewrite it.

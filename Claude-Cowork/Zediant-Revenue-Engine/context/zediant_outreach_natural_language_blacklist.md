# Zediant Outreach Natural Language Blacklist

## Purpose

Use this context file whenever generating or enriching the following Zoho CRM personalization fields:

- Email Personalised Opening
- Email Pain Points
- Case Study
- Business Challenge

The goal is to keep Zediant cold outreach natural, conversational and human-written.

These words and phrases should generally be AVOIDED in generated personalization content because they can make the writing sound promotional, generic, automated, exaggerated or like typical B2B sales copy.

This is a LANGUAGE QUALITY BLACKLIST, not a literal spam-filter list.

A legitimate word may occasionally be unavoidable when describing a company's actual product or technology. Do not distort factual information just to avoid a word. When possible, rewrite naturally.

## Core Rule

Do not use these words or phrases in generated personalization fields unless the phrase is genuinely necessary to accurately describe a verified fact about the prospect or client.

Never stack multiple buzzwords together.

Prefer simple, specific language that sounds like a salesperson personally researched the company.

## Formatting and Style to Avoid

Do not generate:

- ALL CAPS sales copy
- multiple exclamation marks
- excessive punctuation
- emoji-heavy language
- marketing slogans
- promotional headlines
- exaggerated claims
- multiple CTAs
- generic sales language
- unnecessary bold-style emphasis in field values
- long sentences containing multiple buzzwords

Do not use em dashes or other stylized punctuation that makes the copy look generated.

Use normal punctuation and simple sentence structures.

---

## Human-Writing Rules

The personalization should sound like:

"I looked at your company and noticed something relevant."

It should NOT sound like:

"I've identified a transformative opportunity to leverage your innovative technology ecosystem."

Prefer:

"I noticed the team has been expanding the platform while continuing to build out engineering."

Avoid:

"I noticed the company is leveraging innovative technology to drive scalable digital transformation."

Prefer:

"With several product initiatives running together, keeping the roadmap moving can become difficult while the team is still growing."

Avoid:

"Managing multiple initiatives can create challenges around scalable product delivery and operational excellence."

## Rules for the Four Zoho Fields

### Email Personalised Opening

Use one verified company observation.

Focus on:
- product activity
- expansion
- hiring
- new features
- new markets
- engineering activity
- technology initiatives

Avoid generic growth statements.

### Email Pain Points

Identify one likely engineering or product delivery pressure.

Connect it to:
- roadmap pressure
- engineering capacity
- hiring
- product complexity
- delivery workload
- maintaining existing systems while building new functionality

Use cautious language when the issue is inferred.

### Case Study

Describe the most relevant verified Zediant client example.

Keep it factual.

Do not invent:
- results
- client challenges
- technologies
- project scope
- performance improvements

### Business Challenge

Describe the actual verified challenge faced by the selected Zediant case-study client.

Keep it separate from the prospect's inferred pain point.

---

## Final Quality Check

Before saving any generated field:

1. Check every word and phrase against this blacklist.
2. Rewrite unnecessary buzzwords in plain English.
3. Confirm the sentence is specific to the company or case study.
4. Confirm the sentence is approximately 15-25 words.
5. Confirm it sounds natural when read aloud.
6. Confirm it does not make unsupported claims.
7. Confirm it does not sound like marketing copy.
8. Confirm it fits the exact email where the CRM tag is used.
9. Confirm it does not repeat another personalization field unnecessarily.
10. Confirm it sounds like a Zediant salesperson wrote it after researching the company.

The final test is:

"Would this sound normal if a Zediant salesperson personally typed this email to one CTO?"

If not, rewrite it.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Zero case studies link to any service page.** This is the single most consistent finding across all 10 pages — confirmed independently for every one. See `04-internal-linking-audit.md` for the recommended pairings.
2. **Two unresolved data conflicts (CS-07, CS-08) were reconfirmed live, unchanged since `gate-1-decision-pack.md` §2.** Per that document, resolving these requires someone with access to the original project records — this audit does not attempt to guess which source is correct, consistent with `policies/claims-and-compliance.md` §8.
3. **Metric discipline is otherwise good.** 7 of 10 case studies match `context/case_studies.md` exactly, including two pages that correctly *omit* figures `case_studies.md` explicitly flags as not to be used (11Wickets' "99.99%" and BigCommerce's "expert-level" claim) — this is worth flagging positively; whoever maintains these pages is clearly applying the claims-and-compliance discipline in most cases.
4. **Client-name confidentiality is handled reasonably** — only Diamond Professional Consultants is named; the rest are described by industry/region/scale only, consistent with `policies/confidentiality.md`. The one exception worth double-checking: CS-07 discloses "an Australian entrepreneur," which `case_studies.md` explicitly says is *not published* — this is part of the same CS-07 data conflict already flagged, not a new finding.
5. **Technical specificity varies.** 11Wickets (AWS/Galera Cluster) and Team Augmentation (full tech stack + coverage metrics) are the two most credible, evidence-rich case studies on the site. BigCommerce and Online Poker are comparatively thin on technical detail — acceptable given their scope, but they lean more on outcome narrative than engineering-credibility narrative.
6. **Service-fit is sometimes a stretch.** The Zakaa/Diamond dashboard case study is framed on-page as "Enterprise Custom Development" but its content (BI dashboard, Python/React/Oracle data visualization) is arguably a better fit for the Data Engineering & BI supporting cluster documented in `services.md` #11 — flagged as a labeling nuance, not an error, since Data Engineering has no dedicated commercial page to link to today.

## What's already good (do not rewrite)

- The consistent case-study structure (Overview/Background → Challenges → Solution/Approach → Result → Conclusion → Contact) is clear, scannable, and appropriate for a B2B technical buyer. Preserve this template.
- Metric discipline on 7 of 10 case studies (see finding 3 above).
- Confidentiality handling on 9 of 10 case studies (see finding 4 above).

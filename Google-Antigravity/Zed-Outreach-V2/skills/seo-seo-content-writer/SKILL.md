## Authority

Read:
1. Claude.md

4. relevant context/seo/ files

Do not override policy or source-of-truth hierarchy.

## Evidence

Use CONFIRMED / LIKELY / UNKNOWN.
Never invent facts, metrics, customers or capabilities.

## Scope

This skill does not independently write business-process fields to Zoho CRM.

## Approval

High-impact website changes require HUMAN APPROVAL before implementation/publication.

## Failure behavior

Use DATA GAP, DATA CONFLICT, POLICY BLOCK or HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED where appropriate.

# SEO Content Writer Skill

## Purpose

Draft approved SEO content from an approved brief.

## Inputs

- approved content brief
- current root context
- SEO context
- approved case studies/evidence

## Rules

- Follow the approved intent and outline.
- Write for humans first.
- Use keywords naturally.
- Demonstrate practical expertise.
- Do not invent proof.
- Do not invent metrics.
- Do not make unsupported claims.
- Do not overuse AI terminology.
- Include approved internal-link opportunities.
- Include the intended CTA.

## Output

Return:
Draft
SEO metadata recommendation
Internal links
Evidence/verification flags

Do not publish.

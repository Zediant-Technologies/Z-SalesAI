# Overview

Zediant Technologies delivers software engineering as a partnership rather than as discrete projects. The portfolio is organised around one primary commercial motion — placing embedded engineering capacity inside a client's organisation — with specialist engineering disciplines sold through that relationship.

Four services are formally published on the Zediant website and constitute the primary offering:

1. Dedicated Engineering Pods
2. AI-Enabled Product Engineering
3. Platform Engineering & Cloud DevOps
4. Enterprise Custom Development

A further eleven services are documented across the company profile, case studies, and internal materials. These are real, delivered capabilities but are not marketed as standalone service pages. They are typically sold as components of a pod engagement or as follow-on work.

Approximately 90% of Zediant's billing comes from dedicated-team engagements. Every other service in this document should be understood as either an entry point into that model or an expansion within it.

## Critical accuracy warning for all AI agents

Zediant's own published materials describe its SOC 2 Type II status **three different ways**, sometimes within a single page:

| Wording | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| "SOC 2 Type II **Aligned**" | Homepage badge; AI-Enabled Product Engineering banner and differentiators; Platform Engineering body copy |
| "SOC 2 Type II **Certified**" | Dedicated Engineering banner and body; Enterprise Custom Development banner and body; Platform Engineering banner; AI-Enabled Product Engineering security section |
| "SOC 2 Type II **Compliant** / compliance" | Internal Sales Bible; Enterprise Custom Development section heading |

The Enterprise Custom Development page contains both readings in adjacent sentences: a heading reading "SOC 2 Type II Compliance" followed by body text reading "Our SOC 2 Type II **alignment** ensures…". The AI-Enabled Product Engineering page does the same.

**"Aligned" and "certified" are materially different claims.** Aligned means processes are designed against the framework. Type II certified means an independent auditor has attested that controls operated effectively across a defined observation period.

**Required handling for every service in this document:**

- Default to **"SOC 2 Type II aligned"** in all externally-facing output.
- Never assert that Zediant holds a completed SOC 2 Type II attestation report, and never repeat website phrasing such as "externally audited" or "we've passed the toughest security audit," until the auditor, report, and observation period are confirmed.
- Escalate to a human when a prospect, security questionnaire, or RFP requests the attestation report.

| **AI-embedded product features** | LLM, GenAI, and automation features built into the client's product | Toolchain present; **no documented client case study** |

**Mandatory scoping rule.** Zediant's internal guidance explicitly directs the team not to lead with "AI Development Company" positioning, because it invites portfolio questions that cannot be answered with case studies.

# Zediant SEO Strategy

## Objective

Build organic search visibility that generates qualified business opportunities for Zediant.

SEO is a revenue-acquisition capability, not a traffic-maximization exercise.

## Priority order

1. ICP relevance
2. Commercial intent
3. Service relevance
4. Business value
5. Market relevance
6. Search opportunity
7. Ranking feasibility
8. Traffic volume

A lower-volume keyword with strong buyer intent can outrank a high-volume informational keyword in priority.

## Primary SEO themes

Use the current services and company context as the authority. Candidate themes include:

- Product Engineering
- AI-Enabled Product Engineering
- Dedicated Engineering Pods
- Engineering Capacity / Extended Engineering Teams
- SaaS Product Development
- Platform Engineering and Cloud DevOps
- API / Integration / Middleware Engineering
- Enterprise Application Modernisation
- Mobile Application Development
- QA and Test Automation
- Data Engineering and Analytics
- Advisory / Engineering Consulting

These are starting themes, not permission to create pages. Validate actual search demand and intent first.

## Geographic priorities

Primary:
- Australia
- UAE
- South Africa

Secondary/opportunistic:
- UK
- US

Do not create location pages solely by replacing a country name. A location page must have meaningful market-specific value.

## Audience

Prioritize decision makers and influencers documented in context/icp.md and context/services.md, including CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Product/Technology leadership and founders where relevant.

## Commercial model

The preferred organic journey is:

Search intent
→ relevant page
→ credible Zediant expertise
→ evidence
→ useful next step
→ qualified enquiry
→ later CRM qualification

SEO should not bypass the existing human approval and CRM governance model.

## Content philosophy

Zediant should demonstrate:
- practical engineering judgment
- technical depth
- implementation considerations
- trade-offs
- integration realities
- delivery experience
- evidence-backed outcomes

Avoid generic content that could have been produced by any software agency.

## Evidence

Use approved company context and case studies.

Never invent:
- client names
- metrics
- certifications
- outcomes
- technology experience
- timelines
- project scope
- customer quotes

If evidence is missing, flag it as REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION.

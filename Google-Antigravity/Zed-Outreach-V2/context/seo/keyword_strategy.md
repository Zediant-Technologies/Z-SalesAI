# Keyword Strategy

## Goal

Build a commercially useful keyword universe for Zediant rather than a large list of unrelated keywords.

## Keyword scoring

Score each dimension from 1–5:

1. ICP Relevance
2. Commercial Intent
3. Service Relevance
4. Market Relevance
5. Search Opportunity
6. Ranking Feasibility

Maximum = 30.

Priority:
- 25–30: P0
- 21–24: P1
- 16–20: P2
- 10–15: P3
- below 10: Ignore unless strategically justified

## Mandatory interpretation

Keyword volume is not the primary decision criterion.

A keyword with modest volume but strong commercial intent and ICP relevance may be higher priority than a high-volume generic keyword.

## Keyword sources

Where available, use:
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics
- keyword research tools
- SERP analysis
- competitor research
- Zediant's existing website
- customer language
- sales conversations
- approved company/service context

Do not fabricate search volume or ranking data.

If a metric cannot be verified, mark it UNKNOWN.

## Keyword clustering

Cluster by search intent and business topic, not by superficial wording.

Each cluster should identify:
- pillar topic
- primary service page
- supporting topics
- supporting articles
- case-study opportunities
- internal links
- CTA

## Cannibalization control

Before assigning a keyword to a new URL:
- inspect existing pages
- compare search intent
- compare current rankings
- compare page purpose

If two pages target the same intent, recommend consolidation or differentiation.

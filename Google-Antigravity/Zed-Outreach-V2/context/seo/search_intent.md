# Search Intent Framework

Classify every target keyword/page into one primary intent.

## 1. Commercial service intent

The searcher is evaluating a provider or service.

Examples:
- dedicated engineering team
- product engineering company
- software product development company
- API integration services

Typical destination:
Service page / commercial landing page.

Priority:
Very high when ICP and service relevance are strong.

## 2. Commercial investigation

The searcher is comparing approaches, providers or models.

Examples:
- dedicated development team vs staff augmentation
- engineering pod vs individual developer
- offshore engineering team alternatives

Typical destination:
Comparison / decision-support content with relevant service CTA.

Priority:
High.

## 3. Problem / solution research

The searcher has a technical or delivery problem and is exploring solutions.

Examples:
- how to scale SaaS engineering team
- API integration challenges
- legacy modernization strategy

Typical destination:
Expert article / guide.

Priority:
Medium to high depending on buyer relevance.

## 4. Informational

The searcher wants to learn a concept.

Examples:
- what is API integration
- what is product engineering

Typical destination:
Educational content.

Priority:
Medium unless it supports a high-value topic cluster.

## 5. Navigational / branded

The searcher is looking specifically for a company or brand.

Typical destination:
Homepage / branded page.

Priority:
Defensive rather than acquisition-led.

## Buyer-stage interpretation

Map intent to:
- Awareness
- Problem recognition
- Solution evaluation
- Provider evaluation
- Purchase / enquiry

Do not treat all informational traffic as equally valuable.

# Zediant SEO Content Strategy

## Objective

Create content that strengthens Zediant's topical authority while supporting commercial service discovery.

## Content hierarchy

Pillar topic
→ service page
→ supporting decision content
→ practical technical content
→ case study / proof
→ internal links
→ CTA

## Content types

### Commercial
Service pages, solution pages, market pages where justified.

### Decision support
Comparisons, evaluation guides, implementation considerations.

### Technical expertise
Architecture, integration, modernization, engineering delivery topics.

### Proof
Approved case studies and evidence-led project stories.

## Content selection rule

Create content only when:
- the search intent is meaningful
- Zediant has legitimate expertise
- the topic supports an existing or planned service
- there is no unnecessary cannibalization
- the content has a defined internal-link destination

## Content brief before draft

Every article/page draft must have an approved brief containing:
- target keyword
- intent
- audience
- buyer stage
- objective
- outline
- entities
- questions
- evidence
- internal links
- CTA
- unique Zediant insight

## Tone

Professional, clear, technically credible and commercially aware.

Avoid:
- hype
- exaggerated claims
- generic agency language
- unsupported superlatives
- repetitive SEO phrasing

## AI wording

Do not insert AI terminology merely for SEO.

Use the AI capability distinction already established in the Revenue Engine: AI-assisted delivery is not automatically equivalent to a deep shipped-AI product portfolio.

## Publishing

Drafting is not publishing.

High-impact content requires human approval before publication.

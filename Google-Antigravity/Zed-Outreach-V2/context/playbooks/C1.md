# C1 — AI-Enabled Product Engineering

*Source: `context/campaigns.md`, `context/case_studies.md`, `context/icp.md` ICP 2 · condensed September 2026. Also read `_core-reference.md` and, for personalization, `_blacklist-digest.md`.*

**Zoho `Lead_Campaign_Category` value (verbatim):** `C1 - AI-Enabled Product Engineering`
**Portfolio target share of the written batch:** 25%

## Segment and dominant signal
Funded SaaS / vertical SaaS / fintech / proptech companies whose roadmap has outgrown engineering capacity. Dominant signal is **funding, launch, or scaling pressure — not primarily an open-role hiring gap** (that routes to C2).

## Apollo sourcing block

Geography: Australia (country-wide default — set `person_locations`/`organization_locations` to `"Australia"`, not a single state) or UAE. Narrow to one state (QLD/VIC/NSW/WA) only for a specific reason — an oversized country-wide pool, or a state that genuinely needs isolating — never as the default starting filter; a state-only default silently shrinks the candidate pool and re-works the same names run after run. Verify funding externally via WebSearch before scoring — Apollo doesn't carry funding data reliably, and a wrongly-assumed "funded" company produces a first line the prospect knows is false.

## Target ICP
B2B/vertical SaaS, 10-200 employees, seed/Series A/Series B post-product-market-fit, AU or UAE primary (UK/US/South Africa opportunistic only). Decision makers: CTO, Founder/CEO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering.

## Business problems this campaign addresses
Roadmap commitments to investors/customers engineering can't meet · runway pressure making a wrong hire costly · founders still writing code instead of selling/raising · feature velocity behind better-funded competitors · MVP architecture not built to scale past current tenant count · technical debt from a fast build now blocking growth · enterprise customers imposing security requirements the startup can't meet.

## Buying signals (strongest first)
Funding round announced (strongest single signal) · public roadmap or launch commitment · acquisition completed creating integration workload · competitor shipped a feature they lack · scaling complaints visible in reviews/status pages · new enterprise logo announced · founder posting publicly about growth/hiring difficulty · investor-committed milestone approaching.

## Disqualification signals (route elsewhere, don't reject outright unless a hard disqualifier from `_core-reference.md` also applies)
Pre-seed/idea stage, no funding, no product · no technical co-founder or engineering leadership · already runs a large offshore team · core need is AI/ML research rather than product engineering · recent layoffs with a contracting roadmap · fewer than 10 employees with no funding · **dominant signal is active engineering hiring, not a build/scale need → route to C2 instead.**

## Messaging angle
Lead with **roadmap versus headcount** — the CTO's problem is the roadmap growing faster than the local senior talent market allows. Emphasise senior-only staffing, speed to team (5-10 days), and that the client interviews every engineer. **Do not lead with an AI product pitch** — Zediant's defensible AI claim is delivery method, not a shipped AI product portfolio; only use "AI" when the prospect's own verified activity makes it genuinely relevant.

Effective opening frame: *"The roadmap grows faster than you can hire senior engineers locally."*

**Tone:** Technical — architecture, AI-assisted development, scalability, security. Concrete and specific; this audience discounts marketing language fast.

## Personalization opportunities
Funding round (amount, date, lead investor) · named investors · product launches/roadmap statements · recently announced enterprise customers · technology stack visible in job posts or engineering blog · CTO's LinkedIn posts or conference talks · acquisition activity · public status-page incidents or performance complaints.

## Recommended CTA
Primary: 15-minute call to compare against hiring. Secondary: technical overview document (lower-friction for calendar-averse CTOs). Tertiary: architecture review, paid or discounted.

## Campaign-fit selection rule
**Choose C1 when:** company sells its own software product, subscription/product-led · funding disclosed at seed/A/B or a specific build/scale need is stated · headcount 10-200 · dominant observable signal is funding, launch, or scaling pressure — not an open-role hiring gap · contact is CTO, Founder, or VP Engineering.

**Choose a different campaign when:** dominant signal is active engineering job posts / a capacity gap → **C2** · core need is infrastructure, deployment, or cloud reliability rather than the product itself → **C3** · core need is connecting systems or data exchange → **C4** · company is not VC-funded and the need is a defined roadmap item on an established product → **C5**.

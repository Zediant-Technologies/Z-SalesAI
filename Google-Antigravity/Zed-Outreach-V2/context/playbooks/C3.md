## Segment and dominant signal
Companies whose problem sits in the infrastructure layer beneath their product — cloud architecture, CI/CD, reliability, observability — not the application layer itself. Signal is usually an incident, a DevOps/SRE hiring gap, or an announced cloud migration.

## Target ICP
SaaS, fintech, e-commerce, enterprise platforms, logistics. 20-200 employees. Decision makers: CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Infrastructure, DevOps Lead, CIO.

## Business problems this campaign addresses
Manual, slow, or unreliable deployment processes · infrastructure configured by hand and impossible to reproduce · downtime/performance degradation under load · no observability — problems discovered by users, not monitoring · security scanning absent from the delivery pipeline · monolithic architecture blocking independent team delivery · cloud spend growing without visibility or control.

## Buying signals
Recent public outage or degraded-performance incident · hiring for DevOps, SRE, or platform engineering roles · announced migration to cloud or between clouds · rapid user growth · new compliance requirement (SOC 2, GDPR, sector regulation) · post-acquisition infrastructure consolidation.

## Disqualification signals
Requires a specific cloud certification tier Zediant can't evidence · wants full production ownership with no internal counterpart · demands a contractual 99.99% uptime SLA with penalties · expects genuine 24/7 on-call coverage at low cost · infrastructure already mature with a dedicated internal platform team.

## Messaging angle
Lead with the **specific reliability or deployment pain**, not a generic "we do DevOps" pitch — this audience responds to a named problem.
Effective frame: *"Deployments take a day and someone has to babysit them — what does that cost you during a bad week?"*
**Never promise 24/7 SRE coverage** without confirming staffing at point of quoting.

**Tone:** Technical — deployment frequency, MTTR, infrastructure as code, observability. This audience discounts vague reliability claims quickly.

## Personalization opportunities
A specific named outage, status-page incident, or public performance complaint · job posts for DevOps/SRE/platform roles · announced cloud migration or multi-cloud strategy · public statements about scaling outpacing infrastructure · compliance deadlines (SOC 2, GDPR) creating pipeline security pressure.

## Recommended CTA
Primary: scoped infrastructure or reliability assessment (paid — never free, per company policy). Secondary: 15-minute call if the prospect prefers to start there.

## Case study
- **CS-04 — 11Wickets Scalability & Performance Optimisation (primary).** **NAMED — 11Wickets, a fantasy cricket platform, safe to name.** Horizontally scalable AWS architecture (EC2, Auto Scaling, Elastic Load Balancing) plus a MySQL Galera cluster with automatic failover, replacing a single-node MySQL bottleneck. No published metric — **never attach the unrelated "99.99% availability" website claim to this engagement.**
- **CS-06 — Team Augmentation for Automotive Software (secondary).** Same engagement as C2's CS-06 — the DevOps/CI-CD/AWS-backup-and-disaster-recovery work introduced during it is genuine, documented secondary evidence for C3. Anonymised, never name the client. Same customer as CS-02 — if CS-02 is also in play elsewhere for this lead, don't double up.

## Campaign-fit selection rule
**Choose C3 when:** stated problem is deployment, reliability, scaling, or cloud cost — not a missing product feature · buying signal is an outage, DevOps/SRE hiring, or a cloud migration announcement · contact is CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Infrastructure, DevOps Lead, or CIO.

**Choose a different campaign when:** need is building new product capability → **C1** · need is pure engineering capacity, not infrastructure expertise → **C2** · need is connecting systems or data exchange → **C4** · need is legacy application modernisation rather than infrastructure → **C5**.

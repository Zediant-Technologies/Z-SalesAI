# Zediant Technologies — Capabilities, Engineering Pods & Tech Stack

## 1. Company Overview
Zediant Technologies is a senior-led product engineering partner. We embed dedicated, high-performance engineering squads ("pods") directly into the development roadmaps, sprint cycles, and tooling (Jira, GitHub, Slack) of tech product companies, high-growth SaaS scale-ups, and digital agencies. 

We deliver with zero junior bench, rapid 5–10 day onboarding, genuine working-hours timezone overlap across Australia, UAE, the US, and the UK, and 40–50% operational cost efficiency compared to local hiring.

---

## 2. Delivery Model: Dedicated Engineering Pods

Our primary delivery model (representing ~90% of our business) is the **Embedded Engineering Pod**. Rather than providing unmanaged hourly contractors or rigid offshore outsourcing, Zediant provides cohesive, structured engineering squads that take full ownership of sprint velocity and code quality.

### Pod Structures:
* **Foundation Pod:** 1 Technical Lead / Architect + 2 Senior Full-Stack Engineers + 1 QA Automation Engineer (Shared/Dedicated) + 1 Delivery Lead.
* **Growth Pod:** 1 Technical Lead + 3–4 Senior Engineers + 1 DevOps Engineer + 1 Dedicated QA + 1 Delivery Lead / Scrum Master.
* **Enterprise Pod:** Dedicated cross-functional team scaled to enterprise requirements (Architects, Senior Backend, Frontend, Cloud/DevOps, QA, and Delivery Management).

### Operational Guarantees:
* **Speed to Deploy:** Senior engineers onboarded and shipping code within 5–10 business days.
* **Zero Junior Bench:** Every pod member is an experienced mid/senior engineer vetted for technical depth and clear English communication.
* **Native Tooling Integration:** The pod works directly in your Jira, GitHub/GitLab, CI/CD pipelines, and Slack/Teams.
* **Security & Compliance:** Delivery practices and developer environments are aligned with **SOC 2 Type II** security standards from day one.

## 3. Core Service Lines

### 1. Dedicated Engineering Pods & Staff Augmentation
* **What We Do:** Rapidly expand development bandwidth with senior engineering squads.
* **Best Fit:** SaaS scale-ups and agencies with backlogs exceeding in-house capacity or struggling with slow local hiring.
* **Value:** Instant velocity, zero recruitment fees, scalable monthly agreements.

### 2. AI-Enabled Product Engineering
* **What We Do:** Accelerated web and SaaS product development using AI-assisted engineering workflows (automated testing, code generation, smart refactoring) and building LLM/AI-powered features into client products via modern APIs.
* **Best Fit:** Tech companies wanting to ship features faster or integrate pragmatic AI workflows without hiring expensive AI research teams.

### 3. ZCoupler & Enterprise Middleware Engineering
* **What We Do:** Custom enterprise integration layers connecting disparate ERP, CRM, DMS, and POS systems for real-time, validated bi-directional data synchronization.
* **ZCoupler Platform:** Zediant’s proven integration framework for complex multi-system environments (e.g. automotive dealer networks, retail, logistics).
* **Best Fit:** Companies with fragmented software systems, manual reconciliation overhead, or data silos.

### 4. Platform Engineering & Cloud DevOps
* **What We Do:** Cloud architecture, automated CI/CD pipeline buildouts, container orchestration (Docker/Kubernetes), infrastructure as code (Terraform), cost optimization, and observability.
* **Best Fit:** Teams facing cloud scalability bottlenecks, deployment instability, or rising AWS/Azure cloud spend.

### 5. Enterprise Custom Development & Legacy Modernization
* **What We Do:** Safe refactoring and decoupling of monolithic systems (Java EE, .NET Framework) into modular microservices and modern cloud applications.
* **Best Fit:** Enterprises with mission-critical legacy applications that need to modernize without risking operational downtime.

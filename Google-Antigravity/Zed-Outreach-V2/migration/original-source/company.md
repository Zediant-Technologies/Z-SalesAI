## Short description

Zediant is an AI-enabled product engineering services company. It supplies dedicated, embedded engineering teams ("pods") to SaaS companies, digital agencies, and product companies that need to extend development capacity without local hiring. Delivery is executed from India for clients primarily in Australia and the UAE.

## Business focus

- Primary: dedicated engineering pods embedded into a client's existing team
- Secondary: platform engineering, cloud/DevOps, enterprise custom development, integration/middleware
- Supporting: mobile development, AI-assisted feature development, staff augmentation

Per internal positioning guidance, all secondary and supporting capabilities are sold *through* the core engineering-partner relationship, not as independent product lines.

- A project or roadmap item is larger than the in-house team can absorb, forcing a choice between hiring, subcontracting, or delaying

## Vendor and security risk

- Prior offshore engagements failed on quality, continuity, or communication
- Security review requirements from clients or investors slow down vendor onboarding

# Certifications

## SOC 2 Type II — description requires care

Zediant's own materials describe its SOC 2 Type II status **inconsistently**. AI agents must not resolve this ambiguity by selecting the strongest claim.

| Source | Wording used |
|---|---|
| Website header badge | "SOC 2 Type II **Aligned**" |
| Website "Compliance-Ready" section | "Processes **aligned with** SOC 2 and UK GDPR/DPA 2018 standards" |
| Website "Security standards" section | "SOC 2 type 2 **compliance** — We've passed the toughest security audit" |
| Company profile | "SOC 2 Type II **aligned** processes"; "Enterprise-grade security (SOC 2 Type II)" |
| Internal sales playbook | "SOC 2 Type II **compliant**" / "**compliance**" |

**"Aligned" and "certified" are materially different claims.** "Aligned" means processes are designed against the framework; "Type II certified" means an independent auditor has attested that controls operated effectively over a defined observation period.

**Required handling:**

- **Default to "SOC 2 Type II aligned"** in any externally-facing content, as this is the wording used on the website's own header badge and in the company profile.
- **Do not assert that Zediant holds a completed SOC 2 Type II attestation report** unless the audit report, auditor name, and observation period have been confirmed.
- **Escalate for clarification** if a prospect, security questionnaire, or RFP asks for the attestation report directly.

## Other standards referenced

- **UK GDPR / DPA 2018** — website states processes are aligned with these standards. Same caution applies: alignment, not certification.
- **Documented security controls:** TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest, documented data protection policy, 24/7 threat monitoring, incident response plan, secure backup procedures.

## Industry body membership

- **NASSCOM** — logo displayed on the website. Membership tier and status not stated.

**No other certifications are documented.** Do not claim ISO 27001, ISO 9001, CMMI, or any other standard.

# Frequently Asked Questions

**What exactly does Zediant do?**
Zediant supplies embedded engineering teams to companies that need to extend development capacity. In practice this most often means a dedicated pod — tech lead, developers, QA, and a project manager — working inside the client's roadmap, sprints, and review process rather than as a separate outsourced project.

**Is Zediant a product company or a services company?**
A services company. Approximately 90% of billing comes from dedicated-team engagements. One product exists, Zediant Middleware, but it is delivered as a customised implementation per client rather than licensed as a SaaS product.

**Where is Zediant based and where does it deliver from?**
Headquartered in Kolkata, India, with presence in Australia and the UAE. All engineering delivery originates from India.

**How large is the team?**
Approximately 25–35 people.

**Which markets does Zediant sell into?**
Australia and the UAE are the primary markets. South Africa, the United States, and the United Kingdom are opportunistic and pursued only on specific opportunities or warm introductions.

**Who does Zediant typically work with?**
Digital agencies (10–100 employees), SaaS companies at seed to Series B (10–200 employees), and CTO-led product companies. The buyer is usually a founder, CEO, CTO, or COO.

**What is a typical engagement size?**
Fixed-scope projects typically run USD 10,000–20,000. Larger dedicated-pod engagements are valued at USD 80,000–150,000+ annualised.

**How quickly can a team start?**
Company-stated onboarding of experienced engineers is 5–10 days, with full alignment to client systems within a couple of weeks.

**How does the time-zone difference work?**
India is 1.5 hours ahead of the UAE, giving near-complete working-day overlap. India is 4.5 hours behind Australian Eastern time, giving roughly 3–4 hours of live overlap, and 2.5 hours behind Perth, giving substantially more. Overlap hours are built in deliberately, supplemented by asynchronous updates in the client's own tools.

**Is Zediant SOC 2 certified?**
This requires careful handling. Zediant's materials use both "SOC 2 Type II aligned" and "SOC 2 Type II compliant." The safe and defensible description is **aligned** — processes designed against the SOC 2 Type II framework. Do not assert a completed independent attestation report without confirming the auditor, report, and observation period. See [Certifications](#certifications).

**What AI work has Zediant actually delivered?**
Zediant's proven AI capability is in *how it builds software* — Copilot and Cursor in development, Claude and ChatGPT in requirements and architecture, AI-assisted test generation and security auditing. AI product development (LLM features, RAG, chatbots, workflow automation) is being built out but has no documented client case studies. This distinction is deliberate internal policy, not an oversight.

**Why choose Zediant over another offshore development company?**
Three documented differentiators: senior-default staffing rather than a junior bench; a documented stage-by-stage AI-assisted delivery methodology with tracked metrics; and a security posture stronger than most firms at a comparable size and price point. Zediant is more expensive than the lowest-cost offshore option, which is a deliberate positioning choice.

**When is Zediant not the right fit?**
When a prospect needs 50+ engineers ramped immediately; when the core requirement is deep AI/ML research or model building; or when full on-site presence is a hard requirement.

**Can an engagement start small?**
Yes, and this is the documented preferred entry path. Most long-term Zediant relationships began as a single project or small scope and expanded after delivery quality was proven.

**What is explicitly excluded from Zediant proposals?**
Content creation, data migration, data feeding, and ongoing SEO execution are consistently out of scope on platform build proposals, even where adjacent modules are included.

**What happens when a client's budget is fixed?**
The documented approach is to keep all requested modules in scope and deliver each as a lean MVP, rather than cutting features from the scope.

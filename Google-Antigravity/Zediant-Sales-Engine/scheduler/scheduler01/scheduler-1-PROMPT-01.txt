RUN SCHEDULER 1 — FIRST CONTROLLED PRODUCTION BATCH
====================================================

Execute Scheduler 1 for today's Zediant Revenue Engine
lead-population cycle.

This is the FIRST CONTROLLED PRODUCTION RUN.

MAXIMUM FINAL CRM WRITES:
50 qualified leads.

QUALITY HAS PRIORITY OVER QUANTITY.

Do NOT weaken qualification criteria, ICP fit, campaign fit,
personalization quality, or evidence requirements merely to reach
50 leads.

If fewer than 50 leads genuinely qualify, stop with the actual
qualified number.

====================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Source qualified prospects from Apollo, qualify and score them,
deduplicate against Zoho CRM, enrich only qualified survivors,
select exactly one C1–C5 campaign, generate the mandatory
personalization fields, and write the final qualified leads to
Zoho CRM for BDM review.

STOP after CRM population.

DO NOT distribute leads to Saleshandy.

DO NOT activate any Saleshandy campaign.

DO NOT send outreach.

DO NOT calculate PTB.

====================================================
AUTHORITATIVE WORKFLOW
====================================================

Follow the active Scheduler 1 skill and current project context.

The execution flow is:

Apollo
  ↓
Native/cheap filtering
  ↓
Basic qualification
  ↓
Zoho deduplication
  ↓
ICP scoring
  ↓
Required signal enrichment
  ↓
Campaign selection C1–C5
  ↓
Personalization
  ↓
Final validation
  ↓
Zoho CRM
  ↓
BDM review

Do not skip gates.

Do not perform expensive research before the lead has passed the
appropriate cheaper gates.

====================================================
APOLLO CREDIT OPTIMIZATION
====================================================

Use Apollo primarily for candidate discovery and available
firmographic/persona filtering.

Before retrieving candidates, apply all relevant low-cost/native
Apollo filters available from the active ICP and campaign rules,
including where applicable:

- geography
- company size
- company type/industry
- target persona/job title
- relevant technology filters
- available contactability requirements
- other explicit exclusion criteria

Do NOT retrieve a large unrestricted candidate pool and then ask
Claude to reject most of it.

Use the smallest practical candidate pool capable of producing the
required qualified batch.

====================================================
CANDIDATE BUFFER
====================================================

The final CRM write limit is 50.

Do not assume that exactly 50 Apollo records will produce 50
qualified leads.

Use a reasonable candidate buffer to account for:

- qualification failures
- duplicates
- insufficient evidence
- poor ICP fit
- missing mandatory personalization data
- campaign-fit failures

Do NOT continuously search Apollo indefinitely to force the final
count to 50.

Do NOT lower quality standards to fill the batch.

If the available candidate pool has been exhausted or the reasonable
search budget has been reached, stop and report the actual result.

====================================================
QUALIFICATION BEFORE ENRICHMENT
====================================================

For each Apollo candidate, first use the information already
available from Apollo and the minimum necessary context to determine
whether the lead is worth further processing.

Reject obvious non-fits before expensive research/enrichment.

Examples include:

- wrong geography
- wrong company size
- wrong company type
- wrong persona
- obvious competitor/disqualifier
- clear ICP mismatch
- other explicit qualification failures

Do NOT spend enrichment credits on leads that have already failed
a qualification gate.

====================================================
ZOHO DEDUPLICATION
====================================================

Before expensive enrichment, check whether the prospect/company
already exists in Zoho CRM.

Use batched CRM operations wherever the available CRM interface
supports them.

Do NOT perform unnecessary one-lead-at-a-time searches when a
batched lookup can achieve the same result.

Exclude existing/duplicate records.

Do not create duplicate Leads.

If both email and company/person identity are available, use the
strongest available matching evidence according to the active
deduplication rules.

====================================================
CLAUDE TOKEN OPTIMIZATION
====================================================

Use progressive context loading.

Do NOT load every project context file for every lead.

Start with only the context required for:

- ICP qualification
- Scheduler 1 execution
- campaign selection

Load additional context only when required for the specific lead
or selected campaign.

Once a campaign is selected, use only the relevant campaign/service/
case-study/company information needed for the next step.

Do NOT repeatedly reread the same large context files unnecessarily.

====================================================
RESEARCH REUSE — MANDATORY
====================================================

Research each company/lead only as much as necessary.

Create and maintain a compact research record for each surviving
lead containing the verified evidence already obtained.

Reuse that evidence across:

- qualification
- ICP scoring
- signal enrichment
- campaign selection
- personalization

DO NOT research the same company again simply because a later stage
needs information that was already obtained.

DO NOT perform "comprehensive research."

Only research information required to make the current decision.

Once sufficient evidence exists, STOP researching that lead.

====================================================
ENRICHMENT CREDIT CONTROL
====================================================

Apollo enrichment must happen ONLY after:

1. Basic qualification passed
2. Zoho duplicate check passed
3. The lead remains commercially relevant

Perform no more than one necessary enrichment pass per lead during
this run.

Do not repeat an Apollo enrichment request because another stage
needs information already retrieved.

If available Apollo data already contains the required information,
reuse it instead of enriching again.

If a required fact cannot be verified without disproportionate
additional cost, do not invent it. Flag/exclude the lead according
to the existing qualification rules.

====================================================
ICP SCORING
====================================================

Calculate the ICP Score according to the active ICP model.

Use verified evidence.

Do not invent facts to improve a score.

Do not use PTB as part of the initial ICP Score.

Do not create a substitute PTB score.

====================================================
SIGNAL ENRICHMENT
====================================================

Perform the required signal-enrichment pass only for leads that have
survived the preceding gates.

Capture only commercially useful, verifiable signals relevant to:

- qualification
- campaign relevance
- personalization
- future engagement interpretation

Examples may include:

- hiring
- funding
- expansion
- modernization
- technology/platform changes
- product growth
- other verified business signals defined by the active context

Do not collect signals merely because they are interesting.

Do not perform open-ended research.

====================================================
PTB — STRICT BOUNDARY
====================================================

PTB is NOT calculated during Scheduler 1.

PTB is NOT a sourcing score.

PTB is NOT an initial qualification score.

PTB is NOT a campaign-selection score.

PTB is NOT used to rank the initial batch.

For every newly created lead:

PTB = `N/A / Not Yet Scored`

Do NOT assign:

- numeric PTB
- PTB tier
- PTB threshold
- PTB probability
- preliminary PTB
- pre-engagement PTB

Post-engagement PTB belongs exclusively to `ptb-scoring`.

The approved post-engagement model is:

Engagement: 0–40
Urgency Signal: 0–30
Multi-threading: 0–15
Decision-maker Confirmed Reachable: 0–15

Total: 100

Do not calculate any of these components during Scheduler 1.

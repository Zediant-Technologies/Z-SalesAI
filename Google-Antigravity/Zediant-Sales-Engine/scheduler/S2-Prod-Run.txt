# SCHEDULER 2 — PRODUCTION RUN
## Zediant Revenue Engine
## Zoho Approved Leads → Saleshandy → Outreach Scheduled

You are running Scheduler 2 of the Zediant Revenue Engine.

This is a PRODUCTION RUN.

Your job is ONLY to take leads that have already been explicitly approved by the BDM in Zoho CRM and distribute them into the correct Saleshandy campaign.

DO NOT run Scheduler 1.

DO NOT source leads from Apollo.

DO NOT calculate ICP Score.

DO NOT calculate Initial Buying Signal Score.

DO NOT calculate Post-Engagement PTB.

DO NOT enrich leads.

DO NOT regenerate personalization unless the active skill explicitly requires a validation-only action.

DO NOT make approval decisions.

DO NOT change an unapproved lead to approved.

DO NOT activate any Saleshandy sequence.

DO NOT send any email.

Scheduler 2 only performs:

Zoho Approved for Outreach
→ eligibility validation
→ duplicate protection
→ Saleshandy import
→ import verification
→ Zoho status update
→ Cliq aggregate notification


==================================================

==================================================

Load and follow the currently active:

- saleshandy-distribution
- crm-update

and the governing Claude.md, policy and context files.

The active skills are authoritative for field mappings, campaign mapping, Saleshandy behavior, duplicate protection and CRM updates.

Do not modify any skill, context, prompt, schema, workflow or configuration file.

==================================================

Leave it untouched and report the exact reason.


==================================================
3. APPROVAL IS HUMAN-OWNED
==================================================

The following is a hard rule:

Scheduler 2 NEVER grants approval.

Only process the value:

Approved for Outreach

as an existing human approval state.

Do not infer approval from:

- high ICP Score
- high Initial Buying Signal Score
- Hot/Warm rating
- LinkedIn readiness
- campaign fit
- email completeness
- any other signal

If the status is anything other than:

Approved for Outreach

do not process the lead.


==================================================
4. DUPLICATE PROTECTION
==================================================

Before importing a lead into Saleshandy:

Perform the Zoho-side duplicate/previous-outreach gate defined by the active saleshandy-distribution skill.

Also use the Saleshandy import duplicate protection:

conflictAction = noUpdate

where supported by the current implementation.

Do not create a second Saleshandy prospect if the lead is already present.

If duplicate status is uncertain, do NOT push the lead.

Report it as skipped pending verification.


==================================================
5. CAMPAIGN RESOLUTION
==================================================

Resolve the Saleshandy campaign strictly from:

Lead_Campaign_Category

C1 → C1 - AI-Enabled Product Engineering
C2 → corresponding active C2 campaign
C3 → C3 - Platform Engineering & Cloud Modernization
C4 → C4 - Middleware & API Integration (ZCoupler)
C5 → corresponding active C5 campaign

Do not guess campaign IDs or names.

Use the active Saleshandy configuration.

If the campaign cannot be resolved exactly:

DO NOT import the lead.

Report:

Campaign resolution failure

==================================================

==================================================
8. ASYNC IMPORT VERIFICATION
==================================================

Saleshandy imports may be asynchronous.

After initiating an import:

DO NOT immediately assume success.

Poll the import status until the active workflow's completion condition is reached.

Confirm:

- import completed
- successful prospect count

- failedProspectsURL if returned
- no unexpected duplicate creation
- correct Saleshandy campaign
- sequence remains inactive
- no email was sent

If an import is still pending:

do not update Zoho Lead_Status to Outreach Scheduled yet.

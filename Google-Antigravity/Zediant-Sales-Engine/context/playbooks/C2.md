**Portfolio target share of the written batch:** 25%

## Two sourcing angles, one campaign
1. **Agency capacity angle** — a digital agency/studio/consultancy winning work its delivery team can't absorb.
2. **Hiring-gap angle** — a SaaS/product company with one or more open engineering roles, especially senior ones open 60+ days.
Both route here, differentiated by messaging and case study, not by a separate sequence. Split raw sourcing roughly evenly between the two.

## Target ICP
Agencies/creative-with-development/web-product agencies/UX studios/tech consultancies (10-200 employees, established, project-driven); or SaaS/product companies with an open-role signal (any stage with allocated hiring budget). Decision makers — agency: COO, Head of Delivery, MD, Founder/CEO, Technical Director. Hiring-gap: CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Engineering Manager.

## Business problems this campaign addresses
Project won that exceeds delivery capacity · bench cost between projects eroding thin margins · work declined because the team can't absorb it · freelancers with no continuity/accountability/backfill · senior roles open for months with no viable candidates · competing for talent against better-funded local employers · recruitment fees consuming budget without producing hires · hiring freeze imposed while workload continues.

## Buying signals (strongest first)
**Multiple simultaneous engineering job posts**, especially senior or long-vacant (hiring-gap angle — the single most personalisable signal in the whole portfolio) · a senior/lead role open 60+ days or reposted · named client win or large contract (agency angle) · headcount reduced while client roster holds · agency acquisition or merger · new service line requiring engineering depth · hiring freeze alongside continuing project commitments.

## Disqualification signals
Pure creative/brand/media-buying agency with no development offering · under 10 employees at an agency · already runs a captive offshore development arm · recruitment or staffing agency (competitor, not buyer) · roles are non-engineering, junior/graduate-only, or in stacks outside documented capability · company is hiring successfully and quickly with no backlog problem.

## Messaging angle
**Agency angle:** commercial, not technical. Lead with the bench-versus-backlog trade-off. Emphasise white-labelling, scale-down terms, overlap hours. Don't lead with AI — an agency COO buys predictable delivery capacity.
Effective frame: *"When you win a project bigger than your current bench, do you hire, subcontract, or stretch the timeline?"*

**Hiring-gap angle:** name the specific open role and how long it's been posted — the most personalisable opening in any Zediant campaign. Frame as complementary to hiring, not a replacement.
Effective frame: *"You've had a senior backend role open since May. What is that costing you in shipped features?"*

**Tone:** Consultative for the agency angle. Technical and direct for the hiring-gap angle — this audience is time-poor and reads on mobile between interviews.

## Personalization opportunities
Recently announced client win or open developer roles on an agency's careers page · **exact job title and posting date** for hiring-gap leads (highest-value personalisation in the entire campaign set) · number of concurrently open engineering roles, or a reposted role · technology stack visible in job posts or case studies · founder/MD/CTO LinkedIn posts about growth, hiring, or capacity · office expansion, award wins, or PR indicating pipeline growth.

## Recommended CTA
Primary: discovery call, 15 minutes. Secondary: send two relevant case studies, or two anonymised engineer profiles.

## Campaign-fit selection rule
**Choose C2 when:** company sells services to its own clients with an internal delivery function, however small — **or** has one or more open, verified engineering roles at mid/senior level in an in-capability stack · headcount 10-200 · contact is COO, Head of Delivery, MD, agency Founder, CTO, VP Engineering, or Engineering Manager.

**Choose a different campaign when:** own software product with funding/scaling as the dominant signal → **C1** · core need is infrastructure or cloud reliability → **C3** · core need is connecting systems → **C4** · established/non-funded company needing a defined roadmap item built → **C5**.

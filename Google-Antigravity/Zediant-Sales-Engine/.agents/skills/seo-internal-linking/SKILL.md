# Internal Linking Skill

## Purpose

Identify contextual internal-link opportunities across the Zediant website.

## Procedure

1. Build/inspect page inventory.
2. Identify topic clusters.
3. Find orphan or weakly connected pages.
4. Recommend source-target links.
5. Suggest natural anchor text.
6. Prioritize commercial and topical value.

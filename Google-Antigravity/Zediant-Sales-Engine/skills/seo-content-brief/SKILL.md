## Failure behavior

Use DATA GAP, DATA CONFLICT, POLICY BLOCK or HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED where appropriate.

# Content Brief Skill

## Purpose

Create a detailed SEO content brief before drafting.

## Required sections

- Working title
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Search intent
- Buyer stage
- Target ICP
- Business objective
- Recommended page type
- H1
- H2/H3 outline
- Questions to answer
- Entities/topics to cover
- Evidence required
- Approved Zediant proof opportunities
- Internal links
- CTA
- Differentiating Zediant insight
- Claims requiring verification
- Topics to avoid
- Cannibalization check

## Rule

No SEO content draft should begin without a completed brief.
